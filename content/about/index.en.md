## Our story

Pyronear was born in March 2020, when volunteer engineers and data scientists met during a Data for Good season. They were convinced that artificial intelligence could help firefighters spot fire starts before they get out of control.

Faced with expensive proprietary solutions that small fire services cannot afford, they chose to build a digital commons: open-source early detection, frugal in energy and cost, co-built with firefighters.

## Our mission

To democratize frugal, open and collaborative technologies for fighting wildfires, for the benefit of ecosystems and citizens. In practice:

- **Detect** fire starts as early as possible, so a quick response limits burned areas, emissions and biodiversity loss.
- **Make** this technology affordable for every fire and rescue service, including small rural ones.
- **Share** open knowledge: code, models, datasets and field feedback.
- **Bring together** volunteers, researchers and firefighters around a commons that helps regions adapt to climate change.

## A community

More than 150 volunteers have contributed since 2020: data scientists, developers, designers and firefighters. About fifteen regular contributors, student projects with engineering schools, hackathons and skills sponsorship keep Pyronear moving all year round.

Pyronear is a French non-profit association of general interest, based in Paris.
