Pyronear is a non-profit that runs a community of contributors. Whether you are a volunteer, a company, a foundation, a local authority, a school or a fire service, there is a way to get involved with us. Open the block that fits you.

{{< fold title="Give your time and skills" for="Volunteers, employees through skills sponsorship" >}}
Whether you are a volunteer or an employee of a company that offers skills sponsorship, there is a place for you:

- **AI and data**: improve our detection models, reduce false alarms, grow our datasets.
- **Development**: build the alert platform and the station software. Everything is open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/pyronear).
- **Image annotation**: spot smoke on our camera images to train the model. No technical skills needed.
- **Field experience**: firefighters, foresters, local authorities, installers, your experience helps us choose high points, install stations and fit the tool to real needs.
- **Communication, design, fundraising**: spread the word and give the project the means to grow.

More than 150 volunteers have contributed since 2020. For companies, skills sponsorship lets your teams spend working days on the project.
{{< /fold >}}

{{< fold title="Fund the project" for="Foundations, endowment funds, companies, local authorities" >}}
Foundations, endowment funds, companies and local authorities can:

- **Fund stations** for fire services with limited means.
- **Support open research**: every improvement to our models immediately benefits all stations, in France and abroad.
- **Support us over time**: station maintenance and follow-up, season after season.

To talk about it, [contact us](../#contact-form): we build each partnership with you.
{{< /fold >}}

{{< fold title="Collaborate on teaching and research" for="Schools, universities, labs: let's move forward together on training and research topics in early detection" >}}
We offer concrete projects to students (artificial intelligence, data, software, field work) and work with schools and labs. We already work with Mines Paris and CentraleSupélec.

Do you teach, supervise projects or run a lab? [Contact us](../#contact-form).
{{< /fold >}}

{{< fold title="Spread the word" for="Everyone" >}}
- Tell your fire service, town or local authority about us.
- Put us in touch with owners of high points (water towers, pylons, towers).
- Follow and share our news on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pyronearfr).
{{< /fold >}}
