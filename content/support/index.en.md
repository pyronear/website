Pyronear runs thanks to its volunteers, its partners and the support of committed foundations and companies. This support lets us equip fire services with limited budgets and spend time on open research.

## How to help

- **Volunteer**: data science, development, image annotation or field expertise, every skill helps. Our projects are on [GitHub](https://github.com/pyronear).
- **Donate as a foundation or company**: fund stations and open research.
- **Sponsor skills**: put your team's expertise to work for a digital commons.
- **Spread the word**: tell people about Pyronear and follow us on social media.

Want to get involved? [Contact us](../#contact-form).
