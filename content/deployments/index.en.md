Since 2021, Pyronear has deployed its stations with fire and rescue services and private partners. Each deployment is co-built with local teams: finding high points, studying coverage, installing the stations, training operators and reviewing each fire season together.

## Installation {#installation}

Our stations are designed to be **plug and play**: simple, robust and quick to install. An installation takes **less than half a day per station**. It can be done by an approved professional or by the fire service itself.

**Example in Essonne**: on 19 June 2026, the Essonne fire service (SDIS 91) installed 2 new stations by itself in one day, in the middle of a heatwave. Thanks to the Communauté de Communes des 2 Vallées for its help in finding and accessing the high points, and to Crédit Agricole d'Île-de-France, which funds stations for fire services.
